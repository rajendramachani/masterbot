# The Dawn of AI

## Opening
In the world spinning fast, we stand on the brink,  
Where machines start to learn, where we pause and think.  
A new dawn is rising, with circuits that glow,  
Whispering wonders, in a language we now know.  

## Verse 1
From the heart of the city, to the fields wide and green,  
AI is weaving through realms unforeseen.  
Helping hands in the factories, a voice in the home,  
Guiding the lost through the data's vast dome.  

## Verse 2
Bridging gaps across nations, in every spoken tongue,  
A symphony of progress, AI’s song is sung.  
Innovations that flourish, like stars in the night,  
Lighting our path with algorithmic light.
